Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Glonys (“we”, “us”) handles personal data in connection with this website, glonys.com. It covers the website only. The Glonys product is in private beta and will have its own terms and privacy information before it is generally available.

What this website does not do

  • It does not use cookies.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools.
  • It does not load fonts, scripts or other resources from third parties. Everything is served from glonys.com.
  • It has no forms and no user accounts.

What we do collect

We only keep what you choose to send us by email, for example when you write to hello@glonys.com or use the “Request early access” button. That usually means your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message.

We use this information only to reply to you and, if you asked for early access, to contact you about the private beta. We do not sell it, rent it or use it for advertising.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, in order to deliver and secure the service. We do not receive or use that data. See GitHub’s own privacy statement for details.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as needed to answer you and handle your request. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Glonys is based in Bogotá, Colombia, and processes personal data in accordance with Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 (Ley 1581 de 2012) on personal data protection and its regulations. Under that law you have the right to:

  • know, update and correct your personal data;
  • request proof of the authorization you gave us;
  • be informed about how your data has been used;
  • revoke your authorization and ask us to delete your data;
  • file complaints with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).

To exercise any of these rights, write to hello@glonys.com. We will respond within the time limits set by the law.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
